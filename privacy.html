TTN Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TTN Pro handles personal information when users access our AI-powered video content platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, TTN Pro may process account identifiers, public profile information, video metadata, and information submitted by users.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide authorized features, analyze video trends, support content creation, maintain security, and improve service functionality.

3. TikTok Integration

Where TikTok integration is enabled, TTN Pro accesses TikTok information only through authorized API permissions and in accordance with applicable TikTok developer policies.

4. Data Sharing

TTN Pro may use service providers to operate authorized features. Personal information is not sold to advertisers.

5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as needed for legitimate service purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the official TTN Pro support channel. Users may also revoke third-party authorization through the relevant platform settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, data practices, or legal obligations change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the official TTN Pro support channel.
